Early Life and Family Background

Sharik Khan Jr was born and raised in Achalpur, Maharashtra, India, where his interest in music and creative expression began at an early age. Growing up in a supportive family environment, he developed a strong passion for entertainment, performance, and independent artistry.

His father Naseeruddeen and mother Shirin Parveen have been an important part of his personal journey. He also shares a close bond with his younger brothers Naushad Khan and Irshad Khan Jr, along with his younger sister Uzma Parveen.

This family background and connection to his hometown continue to influence his artistic identity and public image as an independent music artist from Maharashtra.

Education and Academic Journey

During his educational journey, Sharik Khan Jr completed his studies at Rahemaniya Urdu High School, Achalpur, Maharashtra. He later continued his education at Jagdamba College, Achalpur, Maharashtra.

Although he remained connected with academics, his long-term interest gradually shifted toward music, performance, digital creativity, and entertainment-related work. Over time, he began focusing more seriously on building an identity in the independent music industry.

Beginning of an Independent Music Journey

Like many independent artists in India, Sharik Khan Jr started his journey without the backing of major record labels. Instead, he relied on digital platforms, audience support, and consistent content creation to establish visibility.

His music style reflects local culture, public experiences, and regional identity, which helped him connect with audiences across digital platforms. As independent music continued growing in popularity, his songs gradually reached wider audiences through streaming services and social media exposure.

Some of his recognized music releases include Are Kya Bolte Meri Achalpur Ki Public , Shakir Dada Aarahe Hai released on November 25, 2025, and Achalpur Ka King released on December 10, 2025.

In 2026, his release Iran Ka Nara Palestine Ka Nara Masjid e Aqsa Hai Hamara also attracted significant public attention online and further strengthened his digital music presence.

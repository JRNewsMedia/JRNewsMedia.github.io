Independent Music Journey Continues Growing

Born as Kaleemuddeen and professionally known as Sharik Khan Jr, the artist has continued building recognition through independent music releases and audience-focused content. Since childhood, he aspired to become an artist and remained committed to developing his career despite various challenges.

He completed his education at Rehmaniya Urdu High School and Jagdamba College in Achalpur, Maharashtra. Over time, his passion for music, entertainment, and public engagement helped shape his identity as an independent creator.

Today, his work reaches audiences through streaming platforms, artist profiles, entertainment websites, and social media communities across India and beyond.

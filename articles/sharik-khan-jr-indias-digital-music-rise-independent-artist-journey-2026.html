From Achalpur to Growing Digital Recognition

Born and raised in Achalpur, Maharashtra, Sharik Khan Jr developed an interest in music and performance from an early age. He completed his education at Rehmaniya Urdu High School, Achalpur, Maharashtra and later studied at Jagdamba College, Achalpur, Maharashtra.
